# STFT and Spectrograms

Standard Fourier Analysis answers:

"What frequencies exist?"

STFT answers:

"What frequencies exist and when?"

X(m,ω)=Σx[n]w[n−m]e^(−jωn)

## Resolution Trade-off

Large Window:
- Better frequency resolution
- Worse time resolution

Small Window:
- Better time resolution
- Worse frequency resolution

ΔtΔf ≳ constant
